# Broad Organizational Survey

## Familiarity and Operational Readiness for Innovative Clinical Trial Designs

## Purpose

This survey aims to understand colleagues' current awareness, familiarity, and perceptions of organizational readiness for the following innovative clinical trial designs:

- **Seamless Phase II/III trials:** Trials that combine Phase II and Phase III activities within one continuous study.
- **Platform trials:** Trials that evaluate multiple treatments within a shared, ongoing trial structure.
- **Multi-Arm Multi-Stage (MAMS) trials:** Trials that evaluate multiple treatments and allow decisions to be made at planned points during the study.

No technical or statistical knowledge is required. We are interested in your awareness, experiences, and perspectives based on your professional role.

**Estimated completion time:** Approximately 5 minutes
